# 📹 Tutor Guide: Uploading Videos to Your Course

## Quick Start

When you upload a video to your course, it will **automatically** be uploaded to YouTube as an unlisted video and embedded securely in your course.

## Upload Steps

1. **Login** to your tutor account
2. **Go to** "My Courses" or "Create Course"
3. **Click** "Add Course Material"
4. **Select** "Video Lesson"
5. **Fill in**:
   - Lesson title
   - Video duration (optional)
   - Choose your video file
6. **Click** "Upload"
7. **Wait** for the upload to complete (may take a few minutes)

## Video Requirements

- **Format**: MP4, AVI, MOV, WMV, FLV, MKV
- **Max Size**: 500 MB (for quick uploads)
- **Recommended**: 1080p or 720p resolution
- **Tip**: Compress large files before uploading for faster processing

## What Happens After Upload?

1. ✅ Video is uploaded to YouTube as **Unlisted**
2. ✅ Video is automatically embedded in your course
3. ✅ Only enrolled students can view the video
4. ✅ Students won't see YouTube branding or your channel name
5. ✅ You can view the video on your YouTube channel

## Privacy & Security

- **Unlisted**: Only people with the link can view (not searchable on YouTube)
- **Enrollment Required**: Only enrolled students can access in the LMS
- **Privacy Enhanced**: Students don't see related videos or comments
- **Your Channel**: Videos appear in your YouTube Studio

## Managing Videos

### Edit Video Details
- Go to your YouTube Studio
- Edit title, description, or thumbnail
- Changes won't affect the LMS embed

### Delete Video
- Delete from YouTube Studio removes it everywhere
- OR delete the lesson from your course in the LMS

### Replace Video
- Delete the old lesson from your course
- Upload a new video lesson

## Best Practices

✅ **Do**:
- Use clear, descriptive lesson titles
- Keep videos under 30 minutes for better engagement
- Test playback after uploading
- Organize lessons in logical order

❌ **Don't**:
- Change video privacy to "Private" on YouTube (will break embed)
- Delete videos from YouTube without removing from LMS
- Upload copyrighted content without permission

## Troubleshooting

**Upload Failed?**
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure file is under 500 MB
- Try a different video format (MP4 recommended)
- Contact support if problem persists

**Video Not Playing?**
- Check if video is still on your YouTube channel
- Ensure video privacy is "Unlisted" (not Private)
- Clear browser cache and refresh

**Need Help?**
Contact support with:
- Course name
- Lesson title
- Error message (if any)
- Screenshot
